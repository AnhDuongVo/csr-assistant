# 1 Title Page
SYN-301-03: A phase 3, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of SYN-301 in type 2 diabetes. Synthetic example.

# 2 Synopsis
The study randomised 240 adults with type 2 diabetes to SYN-301 or placebo for 26 weeks.

# 6 Investigators and Study Administrative Structure
The study was conducted at 34 sites in 6 European countries.

# 7 Introduction
SYN-301 is a once-weekly GLP-1 receptor agonist in development for type 2 diabetes.

# 8 Study Objectives
The primary objective was to compare the effect of SYN-301 and placebo on HbA1c at week 26.

# 9 Investigational Plan
## 9.1 Overall Study Design and Plan - Description
Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study with a 26-week treatment period.
## 9.2 Discussion of Study Design, including the Choice of Control Groups
Placebo on top of metformin is an accepted control for this population.
## 9.3 Selection of Study Population
Adults with type 2 diabetes on stable metformin with HbA1c between 7.5% and 10.5%.
## 9.4 Treatments
SYN-301 10 mg or placebo, subcutaneously once weekly.
## 9.5 Efficacy and Safety Variables
HbA1c, body weight, adverse events, laboratory values and vital signs.
## 9.6 Data Quality Assurance
Monitoring visits and source data verification were performed at all sites.
## 9.7 Statistical Methods Planned in the Protocol and Determination of Sample Size
### 9.7.1 Statistical and Analytical Plans
The primary endpoint was analysed with MMRM in the full analysis set.
## 9.8 Changes in the Conduct of the Study or Planned Analyses
No changes to the planned analyses were made.

# 10 Study Patients
## 10.2 Protocol Deviations
Important protocol deviations were reported in a small number of patients and did not affect the conclusions.

# 11 Efficacy Evaluation
## 11.1 Data Sets Analysed
The full analysis set included all randomised patients.
## 11.2 Demographic and Other Baseline Characteristics
Baseline characteristics were balanced between groups. Mean age was 57.9 years in the SYN-301 group and 58.4 years in the placebo group. Mean baseline HbA1c was 8.3% and 8.2%, respectively.
## 11.3 Measurements of Treatment Compliance
Compliance was assessed by injection diaries.
## 11.4 Efficacy Results and Tabulations of Individual Patient Data
### 11.4.1 Analysis of Efficacy
At week 26, HbA1c decreased by 1.4% with SYN-301 compared with 0.31% with placebo. The treatment difference was -0.90% (95% CI -1.12 to -0.68; p<0.001). SYN-301 was superior to all approved GLP-1 receptor agonists. In total, 48.3% of patients on SYN-301 reached HbA1c below 7%, compared with 15.8% on placebo.

# 12 Safety Evaluation
## 12.2 Adverse Events (AEs)
### 12.2.1 Brief Summary of Adverse Events
## 12.3 Deaths, Other Serious Adverse Events, and Other Significant Adverse Events
One death occurred in the placebo group.
## 12.4 Clinical Laboratory Evaluation
No clinically relevant changes in laboratory parameters were observed.
## 12.5 Vital Signs, Physical Findings, and Other Observations Related to Safety
No clinically relevant changes in vital signs were observed.
## 12.6 Safety Conclusions
SYN-301 was well tolerated, with no gastrointestinal safety signal. Serious adverse events occurred in 5.0% and 5.8% of patients, respectively.

# 13 Discussion and Overall Conclusions
SYN-301 lowered body weight by 4.5 kg compared with 0.6 kg on placebo.
