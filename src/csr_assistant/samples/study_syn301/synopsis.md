# Protocol synopsis: SYN-301-03 (synthetic)

A phase 3, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 26-week study of SYN-301 10 mg once weekly (a
fictional GLP-1 receptor agonist) added to metformin in adults with type 2 diabetes and HbA1c 7.5% to 10.5%.
240 patients were randomised 1:1. Primary endpoint: change from baseline in HbA1c at week 26, analysed with a
mixed model for repeated measures (MMRM) in the full analysis set. Key secondary endpoints: change in body
weight, proportion of patients with HbA1c below 7%. The study was conducted at 34 sites in 6 European
countries between March 2024 and May 2025.
